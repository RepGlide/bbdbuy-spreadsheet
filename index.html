A Cell Is Not a Promise

Blank Does Not Mean Zero

✓What a cell shows

  • A value currently visible in that cell.
  • A link that points somewhere.
  • An adjacent field that adds meaning.

✕What a cell does not promise

  • A blank is not zero and not "free".
  • A number is not a commitment.
  • A label is not a guarantee about the item.
AnnotationDo not assume a fixed column structure. The Sheet may change, and a missing value stays missing.
Price Needs Context

A Price Is Never Just a Number

Read a price with its context, then re-check it on the actual page.

  • 1

    Currency. A number means nothing without its currency.

  • 2

    Time. A price is a snapshot at a moment, not a live rate.

  • 3

    Version. A price may belong to one variant, not the whole item.

  • 4

    Source. Where the number came from shapes how much weight to give it.

  • 5

    Actual page. The destination page is where the price is confirmed.

Image Context Ladder

Read Images by Level

Each image level has a scope. No single level proves quality or authenticity by itself.

  • 1

    Thumbnail. A small preview; enough to recognize, not to judge.

  • 2

    Product-page image. The seller’s presentation of the item.

  • 3

    Seller image. One seller’s view; not the unit you would receive.

  • 4

    Specific-order photo. Applies to one order, at one stage.

  • 5

    Warehouse photo. What is visible at a moment; still not certification.

Mark the Unknowns

Keep the Unknowns Visible

  • 1

    A blank cell stays blank. Do not turn it into zero or a guess.

  • 2

    A price without currency stays open. Mark it until the currency is known.

  • 3

    An image without a level stays unread. Note which level it belongs to.

  • 4

    A link not yet opened stays unconfirmed. Re-check the destination before continuing.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a BBDBuy spreadsheet?

A BBDBuy spreadsheet is a shared document that compiles listing references, category groupings, and product links formatted for use with agent shopping services. Each row is a reference to read carefully, not a confirmed listing.

Is this website the official BBDBuy service?

No. This website is an independent informational and navigation resource. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by BBDBuy or Google.

Does the row decoder verify products or confirm quality?

No. The row decoder is a reading framework. It does not act as a store, product database, quality certification tool, or live data panel. Missing information stays unknown and is not filled in.

How should I read a price in the spreadsheet?

Read a price with its context — currency, time, version, source, and adjacent fields — and re-check it on the actual page. A price alone, without context, is not a commitment.

How do image references work in a row?

Images are read by level — thumbnail, product-page image, seller image, specific-order photo, or warehouse photo. No single level proves quality or authenticity by itself.

Where do I place an order after reading a row?

After reading a row and re-checking the destination page, you can continue through an agent shopping service to submit the item. This website is a navigation resource and does not process orders itself.
